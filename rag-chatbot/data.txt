DHA City Karachi (DCK) Information Knowledge Base

Overview:
DHA City Karachi (DCK) is a sustainable, green, and smart city project located on the Super Highway (M-9). It is a project by the Defence Housing Authority (DHA) Karachi, ensuring high standards of living and security.

Approval Status:
DHA City Karachi is a fully approved and legal project by the Government of Sindh and is managed by DHA Karachi, which is a statutory body. It is considered one of the safest investment options in Karachi.

Plot Prices (Current Market Estimates):
- 5 Marla (125 Sq Yards): Prices range from PKR 4,000,000 to PKR 6,500,000 depending on the sector and location.
- 10 Marla (250 Sq Yards): Prices range from PKR 7,500,000 to PKR 11,000,000 depending on the sector and development status.
- 1 Kanal (500 Sq Yards): Prices range from PKR 13,000,000 to PKR 19,000,000.

Installment Plans:
DHA City Karachi occasionally offers installment plans for new bookings. Currently, most plots are available on a lump-sum payment basis in the secondary market. However, specific farmhouse projects and newer sectors might have 2-3 year payment plans. Please contact the official DHA office for the latest ballot-based installment opportunities.

Amenities:
- Smart City Infrastructure: Fiber optic connectivity and automated utility management.
- Education City: Dedicated zone for top-tier universities and schools (e.g., IBA, Dow University).
- Healthcare: World-class hospitals and medical centers.
- Security: Gated community with 24/7 surveillance and DHA's dedicated security force.
- Parks and Green Spaces: Over 30% of the area is dedicated to green belts and parks.
- Theme Park and Recreation: International standard amusement parks and community clubs.
- Renewable Energy: Solar parks and wind energy initiatives to minimize carbon footprint.
- Transport: Internal shuttle service and proximity to the M-9 Motorway.
